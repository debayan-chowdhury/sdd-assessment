# Business Requirements

Each entry is numbered sequentially (BRD-001, BRD-002, ...) and captures a requirement
before it becomes a spec. See `.ai-context/specs/` for the artefacts derived from these.

## Entries

- **BRD-001 to BRD-009 — Employee Internal Transfer journey:** see
  [`BRD_Employee_Transfer.md`](./BRD_Employee_Transfer.md) — login/password
  change (all roles), employee-submitted transfer request, and the full
  approval/execution chain (Current Manager, Current HR, Receiving HR,
  Receiving Manager, Payroll, IT, Facilities). All nine entries are
  provisional — no recorded sign-off from a decision-owner.
